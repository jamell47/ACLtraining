ABOUT THIS TRAINING

This training is designed to prepare candidates for attachment, internship and employment by strengthening professional behavior, workplace conduct, and ethical decision-making.

Successful completion demonstrates your readiness to represent yourself, your institution and the Advenware Career Link program with integrity and professionalism.

YOU WILL LEARN ABOUT:

  • Understanding your role as an ACL candidate
  • Professionalism in the workplace
  • Workplace ethics and integrity
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Workplace conduct and responsibility
  • Hierarchy, authority and reporting
  • Handling real workplace situations
  • Professional judgment and decision-making
